Before you write

Many questions are answered in the FAQs. A few quick fixes:

Reminders aren't showing up

  • In iOS Settings → Notifications → HealthWrangler, make sure notifications are allowed.
  • Check that a Focus mode isn't silencing them.
  • Open the appointment and confirm a reminder other than None is selected, and that the appointment is in the future.

iCloud sync isn't working

  • Sign in to iCloud on both devices and turn on iCloud Drive for HealthWrangler (iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Drive).
  • Make sure both devices are on a recent version of HealthWrangler.
  • Open Settings in HealthWrangler and tap Sync Now on each device. The sync file appears in the Files app under iCloud Drive → HealthWrangler.

Moving to a new device or recovering data

  • Use Settings → Full Backup to save a backup file, then Restore on the other device.
  • We can't recover data for you, because we never receive it. Keep a backup of anything important.

Text is too small or too large

  • HealthWrangler follows your iOS text size: Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size.

Reporting a problem

Please include:

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • The HealthWrangler version (shown on the App Store listing)
  • What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Please blur or crop out personal health details.
For your privacy, please don't email us medical records, diagnoses, or other sensitive details. We never need them to fix a problem.
