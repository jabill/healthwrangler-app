Everything for the next appointment

Show up prepared, remember what the doctor said, and keep every family member's details straight.

👨‍👩‍👧

Circle of Care

A profile for you, your kids, your parents, anyone you look after, each with their own appointments, notes, medications, and vaccines.

📅

Appointments & reminders

Schedule visits, pick a provider, and get a reminder the morning of, an hour before, or a day or two ahead. Add to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook.

📝

Pre- and post-visit notes

Start with a checklist of questions to ask, then record what you heard afterward. Attach test results or other documents.

🩺

Providers

Phone, email, address, patient-portal link, and who each provider sees. One tap to call.

💊

Medications & vaccines

Dose, frequency, purpose, lot numbers, and next-dose dates, kept per person and ready to share as a clean PDF.

📤

Share & back up

Share a note or appointment, send a full Care Bundle to another caregiver, export to CSV or PDF, or back up everything to a file.
